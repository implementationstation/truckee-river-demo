Raftirie.com: A Comprehensive Summary for Booking a Rafting Adventure

Overall Intention of the Site: 
Raftirie.com serves as the primary online portal for the Raftirie company, dedicated to providing exhilarating and memorable white-water rafting experiences. The website's core intention is to inform potential customers about the various rafting trips offered, facilitate easy booking, and provide essential information for planning their adventure. The target audience appears to be individuals and groups seeking outdoor recreational activities, ranging from novice adventurers to those with prior rafting experience, who are looking for a well-organized and professionally guided experience. The site aims to build trust and excitement by showcasing the beauty of the rafting locations and the thrill of the sport.
